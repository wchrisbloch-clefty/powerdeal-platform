# Permitting Playbook
*Always web-search current TCEQ/LDEQ status before citing. Non-attainment classifications update.*

## The Core Advantage

SOFC = electrochemical conversion, not combustion.
- Zero NOx, zero SOx emissions
- Not a "combustion source" under EPA/TCEQ definitions
- Does not trigger New Source Review (NSR) or BACT requirements
- No NOx offset credits required

For combustion alternatives in non-attainment zones: all of the above apply in reverse.

## HGB (Houston-Galveston-Brazoria) Non-Attainment

**Zone**: 8-county area including Harris, Galveston, Brazoria, Fort Bend, Montgomery, Waller, Chambers, Liberty counties.
**Standard**: 8-hour ozone
**Classification**: Check current — HGB has historically been "Serious" or "Severe." Search TCEQ before citing.
**Implication**: Any new significant NOx emitter requires full NSR review, BACT analysis, and NOx offset purchase.

**TCEQ NSR Timeline for Combustion (HGB)**:
- Pre-application meeting: 1–3 months
- Application preparation: 3–6 months
- TCEQ review: 12–24 months
- Public comment and response: 3–6 months
- Total: 18–36+ months typical
- Risk: Denial if NOx budget is exhausted or BACT cannot be demonstrated

**NOx Credit Market (HGB)**:
- NOx credits are required to offset new emissions in non-attainment zones
- Price: *Always search current market price — has ranged from $5,000–$30,000/ton historically*
- Availability: Limited and declining as the zone becomes more constrained
- SOFC: No credits required

**SOFC Permitting Pathway (HGB)**:
- Standard Permit or Permit by Rule (PBR) — typically 3–9 months
- No NSR trigger
- No BACT analysis
- No offset credits
- No public comment period (standard permit path)

## Baton Rouge Non-Attainment

**Zone**: East Baton Rouge, West Baton Rouge, Ascension, Livingston, Iberville parishes
**Standard**: 8-hour ozone
**Regulatory body**: LDEQ (Louisiana DEQ)
**Timeline**: Similar to HGB — 18–30 months for combustion NSR in the LA industrial corridor

**LDEQ Pathways**:
- Minor source permit: <100 tpy NOx — faster but limited for industrial scale
- Major source NSR: Full review — same timeline constraints as TCEQ HGB
- SOFC: Non-combustion source — minor permit or registration only
