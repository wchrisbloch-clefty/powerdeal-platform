# Account Strategy Builder

A living account plan. Pulls from all other skills. Updated after every meaningful interaction.

## Structure — 10-Section Account Plan

---
**STRATEGIC ACCOUNT PLAN — [COMPANY NAME]**
*Tier: [1/2] | Owner: [BD/AE name] | Last Updated: [date]*
*Current Gate: [G__] | Qualification Score: [X/60]*

---

### Section 1 — Account Overview
- Company profile: industry, size, revenue, facility footprint
- Financial health: credit rating, recent earnings trend, capex cycle status
- Strategic priorities: what is leadership publicly focused on (growth, reliability, ESG, cost)
- Recent news: last 90 days of material events

*Source: web search + earnings transcripts + sustainability reports*

### Section 2 — Power Situation
- Current power supply: utility provider, contract structure, rate schedule
- Grid constraints: substation capacity, interconnection queue position if known
- Existing generation: cogen assets, diesel backup, solar/storage
- Power cost: estimated all-in $/MWh (confirm or estimate with methodology)
- Reliability history: known outages, curtailments, insurance claims

### Section 4 — Decision Process
- Decision type: Capital appropriation / operating lease / board approval level

## Update Protocol

After every meaningful interaction, update:
- Stakeholder map sentiment and last contact
- Pain map confidence levels
- Risk register
- Win strategy milestones

**This plan is only useful if it's current. A stale account plan is worse than no plan.**

## Mutual Next Step

End every account plan session with:
**The single most important action in the next 7 days to advance this account.**
