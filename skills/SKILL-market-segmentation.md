# Market Segmentation Analyzer

Where to focus. Web search required. Scored on three axes: pain intensity, market density, accessibility.

## Step 1 — Define Scope

Establish what's being analyzed:
- **Segment**: Vertical / geography / trigger condition / emerging market
- **Question**: Where to focus? Is a segment worth entering? How to prioritize?
- **Constraints**: Geography, team capacity, existing relationships

## Step 2 — Web Research

Search for:
- "[Segment] power demand Texas 2025 2026" (or relevant geography)
- "[Segment] ERCOT interconnection queue"
- "[Segment] ESG targets emissions 2025"
- "[Segment] expansion [geography] capital investment"
- "[Segment] grid constraints OR power shortage"
- Competitor presence: "[competitor] [segment] win"

## Step 3 — Score on Three Axes

| Sub-factor | Score | Evidence |
|---|---|---|

**Total Score: [X]/30**

### Prioritization Guidance
- 24–30: **Prioritize now** — deploy dedicated BD resources
- 18–23: **Active pursuit** — include in coverage, build relationships
- 12–17: **Monitor** — set trigger events, don't invest heavily yet
- <12: **Park** — return when conditions change

## Step 4 — Segment-Specific Entry Strategy

For any segment scoring 18+, define:

**Beachhead**: The single best entry point (geography, account type, trigger event)

**ICP within segment**: What does the ideal customer look like in this segment specifically?

**Origination channel**: Which channel produces the highest-quality leads fastest?

**Reference account strategy**: What's the first win that unlocks the rest of the segment?

**Competitive moat**: What makes combustion or grid an especially poor alternative here?
