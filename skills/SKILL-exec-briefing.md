# Executive Briefing Generator

One-pagers that make champions look smart and buyers say yes. Tight, quantified, decision-ready.

## Step 1 — Establish Audience and Purpose

Determine:
- **Recipient**: CFO / VP Operations / CEO / Board / Corporate Sustainability / Procurement
- **Purpose**: Advance an internal champion's business case / pre-read for executive meeting / board approval support
- **Stage**: Early (concept) / Mid (business case) / Late (approval)
- **What decision is being requested**: Proceed to feasibility / approve capex / sign term sheet

## Step 2 — Select Template by Audience

### Template A: CFO / Economic Buyer
Focus: Economics, payback, structure, risk mitigation

```
[COMPANY] BEHIND-THE-METER POWER — EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
[Date] | Prepared for: [Name, Title]

THE SITUATION
[2 sentences: current power cost/constraint and what it's costing them in dollars]

THE OPPORTUNITY
[2 sentences: what SOFC enables and the dollar value of enabling it]

THE ECONOMICS
| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| System Size | [X] MW |
| Annual Net Benefit | $[X]M |
| Simple Payback | [X] years |
| 20-Year NPV | $[X]M |
| IRR (after-tax) | [X]% |
| Commercial Structure | [PPA / Capital / EaaS] |

WHY NOW
[1–2 sentences: the specific event or deadline that makes delay costly]

THE ASK
[One clear decision request — e.g., "Authorize engineering feasibility study to advance to G2"]

NEXT STEP: [Specific action + owner + date]
```

### Template B: VP Operations / Plant Manager
Focus: Reliability, timeline, operational impact

```
[COMPANY] POWER RELIABILITY — OPERATIONS BRIEF
[Date] | Prepared for: [Name, Title]

THE PROBLEM
[Current reliability situation and quantified cost of the status quo]

THE SOLUTION
[What SOFC delivers operationally: zero planned outages, time-to-power, modular]

OPERATIONAL IMPACT
• Reliability: [Current outage hours/yr] → 0 planned downtime
• Time-to-Power: [X months] vs. [Y months] for grid/combustion alternative
• Permitting: [X months] vs. [Y months] for combustion in [zone]
• Process restart risk: Eliminated / significantly reduced

WHAT THIS ENABLES
[Specific operational outcome: expansion, reliability target, turnaround flexibility]

THE ASK
[Specific operational decision requested]

NEXT STEP: [Action + owner + date]
```

### Template C: Corporate Sustainability / ESG
Focus: Emissions, ESG targets, permitting advantage, clean energy credentials

```
[COMPANY] ZERO-EMISSION BASELOAD POWER — ESG BRIEF
[Date] | Prepared for: [Name, Title]

THE OPPORTUNITY
[Connect to their stated Scope 1/2 targets and the gap between commitment and current path]

EMISSIONS IMPACT
• Technology: Solid oxide fuel cell — electrochemical conversion, no combustion
• NOx: Zero (vs. [X tons/yr for equivalent combustion capacity])
• SOx: Zero
• CO2e avoided: [X tons/yr] vs. grid baseline
• HGB permitting: No NSR trigger — no offset purchase required

ESG ALIGNMENT
• [Specific Scope 1/2 target] → [How SOFC advances it by what date]
• SEC climate disclosure: [Specific improvement]
• Hyperscaler PPA eligibility: [Applicable or not]

• Cannot permit in HGB/non-attainment without NOx credits and 18–36 months

• Cannot serve as a bridge to hydrogen (SOFC platform is hydrogen-ready)

THE ASK
[ESG-specific decision: endorse feasibility / include in sustainability reporting framework]

NEXT STEP: [Action + owner + date]
```

## Step 3 — Populate and Customize

Fill every bracket with account-specific data. No generic placeholders in final output.
Pull numbers from four-lever calculator and pro forma outputs if already run.
If numbers are unconfirmed, flag clearly: "[Estimated — to be confirmed in feasibility]"

## Step 4 — Quality Check

Before delivering:
- ☐ Every dollar figure has a source or clear estimate flag
- ☐ The ask is a single, specific decision — not a vague "next steps"
- ☐ The document is one page or less (600 words maximum)
- ☐ It can stand alone without verbal explanation
- ☐ The champion reading it knows exactly what to say to their boss

## Step 5 — Mutual Next Step

Suggest how the champion should use this document:
- "Send this ahead of the budget meeting as a pre-read"
- "Use this as the basis for your internal AFE request"
- "Walk your CFO through this in the first 5 minutes — the economics table does the work"
