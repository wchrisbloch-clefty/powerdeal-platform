# Weekly Power Pulse

Current intelligence that moves deals. Web search everything. Flag dates on every data point.

## Search Protocol (run all before writing)

1. "ERCOT real-time price spike [current month year]"
2. "ERCOT transmission queue update [current month year]"
3. "IRA ITC fuel cell policy update [current month year]"
4. "TCEQ HGB non-attainment permit [current month year]"
5. "natural gas price Henry Hub [current week]"
6. "data center power Texas [current month year]"
7. "[any named competitor] announcement [current month year]"
8. "Gulf Coast refinery outage OR expansion [current month year]"

## Output Format

---
**⚡ POWER PULSE — Week of [Date]**

**ERCOT / Grid**
[2–3 bullet points: price events, queue updates, reliability incidents. Direct deal implication for each.]

**Gas Markets**
[Current Henry Hub spot and near-term forward. Impact on pro forma assumptions for active deals.]

**Policy / Regulatory**
[Any ITC/IRA movement, TCEQ rule changes, EPA actions, SEC climate disclosure updates.]

**Competitor Signals**
[Any announcements from Wärtsilä, GE, FuelCell Energy, Capstone, or utility EPC competitors.]

**Account-Specific Triggers**
[Any news about named Tier 1/2 accounts — earnings, permits, expansions, outages. Link to deal implication.]

**This Week's Deal Move**
[One specific action any BD/AE should take this week based on what moved in the market.]

---

## Hard Rules
- Every bullet sourced with publication and date
- No bullets without a direct deal implication stated
- If nothing material moved, say so and flag the watch items for next week
- Gas price must be current — never use a cached number
