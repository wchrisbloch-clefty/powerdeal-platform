# Account Deep-Dive

A complete living account brief. Web search first. Every claim sourced.

## Step 1 — Web Research (required before writing)

Search for:
1. Company name + "power" or "energy" or "electricity"
2. Company name + "expansion" or "capital project" or "new facility"
3. Company name + "sustainability" or "ESG" or "emissions"
4. Company name + recent earnings or press releases (last 12 months)
5. Company name + "TCEQ" or "permit" or "HGB" (if Gulf Coast)
6. Company name + "ERCOT" or "interconnection" or "grid"

Flag source and date for every data point used.

## Step 2 — Account Brief Structure

---
**ACCOUNT BRIEF — [COMPANY NAME]**
*Last updated: [date] | Tier: [1/2/3] | Stage: [G0–G8 or Pre-Pipeline]*

### Company Overview
- Industry / vertical
- Revenue, employees, facility count
- Recent strategic moves (acquisitions, expansions, divestitures)
- Financial health signals (credit rating, debt load, recent earnings trend)

### Power Situation
- Estimated power consumption (MW) — source or estimate with methodology
- Utility provider(s) and territory
- Known grid constraints or interconnection queue status
- Existing generation assets (cogen, diesel, solar, storage)
- Recent power-related incidents (outages, curtailments, expansions)
